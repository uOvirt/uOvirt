What is uOVirt?

uOVirt is an interactive map of the University of Ottawa campus made by students, for students.

Who can use uOVirt?

Anyone! It is made for students, but visitors and staff can use it too.

Can I reserve a study room?

Yes. With the Student or Pro plan you can see and book study rooms on campus.
