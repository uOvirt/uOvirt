Overall Look and Feel of uOVirt
===============================

The overall look and feel should be clean, simple, and flat. We will avoid 3D buttons, shadows, and glossy effects.
Each page uses white rounded cards on a warm beige background, with a short title and a red button at the bottom to go to the next page.
The website should feel friendly and helpful, like a classmate showing you around campus. Our goal is to get students "unlost" quickly and without stress.

Colours
========
For uOVirt, our colours are inspired by uOttawa's red and a warm, paper-like background that feels like a campus map.
These are our colour swatches:

- #C8321E (uOVirt red, for buttons, highlights, the active menu link and accent words)
- #F4F1EA (warm beige, for page backgrounds)
- #FFFFFF (white, for the top bar, cards and forms)
- #1A1A1A (near black, for headings and main text)
- #777777 (grey, for the footer and small notes)

Iconography
===========
We will use simple, flat icons (like a map, a magnifying glass, an envelope, LinkedIn and GitHub) that match the overall look and feel.
When selecting an icon, please keep the following in mind:

- Pick the icon that clearly matches its job (a map for the campus map, an envelope for email)
- Keep icons the same size and style on the same page
- Use red icons only, so they match our colours
- If a team member has no photo yet, show the grey person icon instead

Typography
==========
We will be using one font from Google Fonts:

- DM Sans, for all text (titles, paragraphs, labels and buttons)
- Titles are bold
- One word in a title can be highlighted in red italics, like "unlost."

Links
=====
Links will not be underlined.
Menu links are black and turn red when the user hovers over them. The link for the page you are on stays red.

Buttons
=======
Buttons will be flat and rounded (pill-shaped), with a red background (#C8321E) and white text.
Every page has a button at the bottom that takes the user to the next page (Home → Features → Pricing → Team → Questions → Home).
Buttons can include a small arrow (→) to show the user they are moving forward.
