Overall Look and Feel of uOVirt
===============================

The overall look and feel should be clean, modern, and flat. We will avoid 3D buttons, shadows that look heavy, and glossy effects.
The website should feel friendly and helpful, like a classmate showing you around campus. Our goal is to get students "unlost" quickly and without stress.

Colours
========
For uOVirt, our colours are inspired by uOttawa's red and a warm, paper-like background that feels like a campus map.
These are our colour swatches:

- #C8321E (uOVirt red, for buttons, highlights and accents)
- #A82815 (dark red, for hover states)
- #F4F1EA (warm beige, for page backgrounds)
- #FFFFFF (white, for cards and forms)
- #1A1A1A (near black, for headings and main text)
- #777777 (grey, for small notes and labels)

Iconography
===========
We will use simple, flat line icons (like a map pin, envelope, and arrows) that match the overall look and feel.
When selecting an icon, please keep the following in mind:

- Pick the icon that clearly matches its job (a map pin for locations, an envelope for email)
- Keep icons the same style and thickness across all pages
- Use red or black icons only, so they match our colours

Typography
==========
We will be using fonts from Google Fonts:

- Headings: Inter Tight (bold and tight, for big titles)
- Accent words: Fraunces Italic (for a highlighted word in a title, like "unlost.")
- Body text: DM Sans (for paragraphs, labels and buttons)

Links
=====
Links will not be underlined and will be bold black text that turns red when a user hovers over it.
The only exception is email links, which have a red underline so they stand out.

Buttons
=======
Buttons will be flat and rounded (pill-shaped), with a red background (#C8321E) and white bold text.
When the user hovers over a button, it turns dark red (#A82815).
Buttons can include a small arrow (→) to show the user they are moving forward.
